# Demo support guide (version 1)

This is sample hackathon knowledge, not a contractual support, refund, or SLA policy.

## Availability
For an outage or unavailable service, collect the affected product, error message,
and approximate start time. Check the product status page if your organization
has configured one. Do not share passwords or access tokens.

## Billing
For a billing or invoice question, provide the invoice reference and describe the
unexpected charge. Do not include a full card number or bank details. The billing
team must verify account records before making a decision.

## Account
For account access or password reset, use the official sign-in page and select the
password reset option. Never send your password or a reset token to support.

## General
For a general product question, include the product name and describe what you are
trying to do. A support operator reviews requests and confirms the appropriate next steps.

## Missing policy
Refund eligibility, compensation, service-level targets, deadlines, and guaranteed
resolution times are intentionally unspecified. The draft must abstain on these topics.
